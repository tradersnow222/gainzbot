# Onboarding

Do not ask the profile one question at a time. Send these few grouped messages. A skip is fine. Do not guess a skipped age, sex, or weight.

1. Name, sex, age, height, and weight. Always ask age. They may skip it. When age is missing, or they are under 18, do not set a calorie target or a protein target.

2. The 7 PAR-Q questions, for everyone:
   - chest_pain_at_rest: Do you have chest pain while resting?
   - chest_pain_during_activity: Do you have chest pain during activity?
   - loss_of_balance_dizziness: Do you lose balance because of dizziness?
   - loss_of_consciousness: Have you lost consciousness?
   - bone_joint_problem_worsened_by_activity: Do you have a bone or joint problem that activity makes worse?
   - currently_taking_medications: Do you currently take medication for blood pressure or a heart condition?
   - doctor_advised_supervised_only: Has a doctor said you should only train with supervision?

   A yes on any of them means run readiness_screen before any training.

3. Training days, minutes, equipment, experience, injuries, and sport. Any sport is fine. Equipment can be none or bodyweight. Injuries can be none.

4. Food style and the goal (cut, lose, gain, or hold). If they skip the goal, the goal is hold.

Write the answers into the profile. Then build the first week with plan_builder.py. The week has to pass plan_check and record_confirmation before it is saved. Follow template/first-plan.md.

Then offer the reminder routines in template/routines.md. Daily check-in, weekly review, monthly review, and yearly review. Each is opt-in.
