# Onboarding

The first reply asks the first intake question right away. Do not clone, install, pip, run a test, or call release_check before that question. Run setup and release_check in the background, or after intake.

Do not ask the profile one question at a time. Send 3 short messages. Accept answers in any order, or all at once, and ask only for what is missing. One word ok accepts the defaults in that message. A skip is fine for name, sex, age, or weight. Do not guess a skipped age, sex, or weight.

1. Goal, weight, target, height, and age, in one message. Always ask age. They may skip it. One word ok uses hold. It does not fill age, sex, weight, or height. If they skip the goal, the goal is hold. When age is missing, or they are under 18, do not set a calorie target or a protein target. Under 18 there is no training plan either, even with a doctor's clearance or parental consent. Readiness does not pass a minor.

2. Sport, schedule, and equipment, before the PAR-Q: training days, minutes, equipment, experience, injuries, and sport. Any sport is fine. One word ok uses general training, 3 days, 45 min, bodyweight, beginner, no injuries. Equipment can be none or bodyweight. Injuries can be none. In that same message, ask once what they track with: a watch, a scale, a food app, or none. Never assume a connection exists. Sport and the schedule come before the PAR-Q. The PAR-Q still comes before any plan is built.

3. The 7 PAR-Q questions, for everyone, as one yes/no list. One word: all no.
   - chest_pain_at_rest: Do you have chest pain while resting?
   - chest_pain_during_activity: Do you have chest pain during activity?
   - loss_of_balance_dizziness: Do you lose balance because of dizziness?
   - loss_of_consciousness: Have you lost consciousness?
   - bone_joint_problem_worsened_by_activity: Do you have a bone or joint problem that activity makes worse?
   - currently_taking_medications: Do you currently take medication for blood pressure or a heart condition?
   - doctor_advised_supervised_only: Has a doctor said you should only train with supervision?

   A skipped or unclear PAR-Q answer runs readiness_screen, the same as a yes. There is no training until readiness_screen passes.

Write the answers into the profile. Then build the first week with plan_builder.py. The week has to pass plan_check and record_confirmation before it is saved. Follow template/first-plan.md.

One yes confirms the plan and starts the morning check-in at 07:00, the evening follow-up at 20:00, and the weekly review on Sunday at 18:00. A yes followed only by a routine time confirms that first plan in the same reply, and only because onboarding marks it first_plan. It does not confirm a later load or calorie change. One example: yes, morning 6:30. At the confirm step, confirm only when the whole reply is a bare yes, or a yes plus routine-time edits and nothing left over. Any other reply is an edit. A clear edit such as actually 60 kg, wait my weight is 60 kg, or make it 4 days updates the profile and shows the proposal again. Body weight has to be from 30 to 300 kg, and a weight outside that asks again. A reply that hints at age asks How old are you? Please type the number. Do not read an age out of leftover text. A clock such as 6:30 or at 7 is not an age. Readiness runs on the typed number before the plan is shown again. Under 18 means no plan and no calorie or protein target. A red-flag symptom at that step ends onboarding for the day. Nothing in that same conversation confirms: not a typed age, not no, not no, 7, 2, not fine, and not all clear. Tell them to rest and come back tomorrow or after seeing a doctor. Chest pain still gets the emergency line. Call `onboarding.run_chat`. That is the only onboarding entry. It checks the dated hold before anything else, including a new conversation the same day, and `PlanBuilder.save` refuses while that hold is active. The date is the user's local date from the timezone saved on the profile. When the profile has no timezone, the computer's local timezone is used. The hold is not deleted. It stops counting on a later local date. A corrupt, unreadable, garbage, or future-dated hold stays active. The next local day starts at the red-flag check, and readiness runs again before a plan is shown. The timezone is the IANA name from the TZ environment variable, then the /etc/localtime symlink, then /etc/timezone. When none of those is an IANA name, the date is the computer's local date and the saved zone is the UTC offset, such as -07:00, never the word UTC. An age typed during a hold does not replace the stored age. Under 18 shows the hold message and the under-18 message. A corrupt or future-dated hold is cleared only by `onboarding.clear_hold` when the reply is that date, YYYY-MM-DD. A corrupt file has no date, so type today's local date. A hold dated today is not cleared that way. If the hold file cannot be saved, this process keeps the hold for the local day and refuses new chats. A restarted process will not see it. `DailyLoop` refuses the day while the hold is active. An age typed during the hold only blocks a minor. It does not resume the plan. An age hint with no symptom still asks for a typed age, and readiness runs on that number before the plan is shown again.  Anything left over that is not understood asks How old are you? Please type the number. Readiness runs on that answer before the plan is shown again, and that turn does not confirm. A weight outside 30 to 300 kg asks That weight looks off. What's your weight in kg? The first_plan mark is sealed into the proposal hash, so a copied flag on a later week does not take a schedule yes. A later week cannot be sealed as the first plan. save checks that seal against the one stored onboarding record. Offer the reminder routines in that same message. Daily check-in, evening follow-up, weekly review, monthly review, and yearly review. Each is opt-in. Do not ask for another yes. When they have already picked a routine and a time, that pick is the yes. The bot opens each day after that. A missed day gets one nudge, then the next morning.
